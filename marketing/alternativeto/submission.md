## Submission notes and blockers

1. Sign in or create an AlternativeTo account and verify the email address. This cannot be done on the user’s behalf.
2. Open the user menu → **Suggest new application**.
3. Paste the fields above and upload the icon and screenshots.
4. The free review queue is described by AlternativeTo as several thousand apps long with no promised date. An optional paid priority review is offered after submission; the exact price is shown at checkout. Do not pay without the account owner’s approval.
5. Do not put links, email addresses or phone numbers in either description. AlternativeTo rejects descriptions containing them; use the dedicated URL fields.
6. After approval, open the Soundpad and Voicemod pages → **Contribute to this page** → **Suggest Alternatives**, then select Voxora.
7. Optional: request developer access to the listing so future edits go live without review.

## Naming decision (important)

The task supplied `https://github.com/ObsQrr/Voxora` together with “Technology: Rust”. Those do not match:

- `ObsQrr/Voxora` is the original **C# / WPF** edition, version 2.2.1, MIT.
- `ObsQrr/VoxoraRust` is the **Rust** edition, version 1.0.0, MIT; it is the edition documented here.

AlternativeTo prefers one entry per product, with editions described in the text. This submission uses the product name **Voxora**, points the website/download fields at the Rust edition, and describes the C# edition in the full description. If a different official URL is preferred, change the Website field only; the rest of the profile remains valid.
